Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026.

Quick answer

We only get what you send us by email or phone. We use it to reply to you. We do not sell personal information. Email us to see, correct, or delete what we have.

This policy covers swyftbb.com and its preview copy, operated by Swyft Solutions LLC ("Swyft", "we"). Swyft Business Broker is an offer of Swyft Solutions LLC.

What we collect

This is a static website with no accounts and no database. The contact form does not send anything to a server. It opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. When you email or call us, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, business name, email, phone number, whether you are a buyer or seller, and your message.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and continue the conversation you started.
  • To keep a record of that conversation in our email and business files.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses and browser details when pages are requested, under its own privacy terms. This site does not add analytics, ad trackers, or cookies of its own.

Your choices

To ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us, email colin@swyft-hq.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
