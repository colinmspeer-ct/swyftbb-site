For buyers

Buy the business. Then grow it.

A new owner has to learn the operation and grow it at once. Swyft HQ can take on the selling and some of the operational work so you can focus on running it.

Quick answer

After you buy a home-service or related business, Swyft HQ can deploy a door-to-door team to sell the service on your paperwork, and Swyft International can place offshore staff for appointment setting, support, bookkeeping, or admin. Tell us what you are looking for. We do not run a marketplace or a list of deals.

What Swyft HQ can do after the purchase

Two services, both live on Swyft HQ today.

01 ↗

Door-to-door sales teams

Swyft recruits, trains, houses, and manages reps who sell your service on your paperwork. You keep the brand, the customer, and fulfillment. You pay a revenue share per sold account, at a rate written in the agreement. Weekly report of doors, conversations, and sold accounts by rep.

How it works on Swyft HQ 02 ↗

Swyft International placements

Offshore staff for work that does not need a truck: appointment setting, customer support, bookkeeping, and admin. The success fee is 33% of first-year salary, and you pay the salary. A $500 deposit applies only to start a new search when the existing database has no fit. 90-day free replacement.

Swyft International on Swyft HQ
03

Sold to your capacity

A new owner often does not know yet how much the crews can absorb. You set the line, and the team sells up to it. Door-to-door work stays with the D2D teams; International covers the office seats.

How it works

Simple on purpose

No buyer scorecard, no gated deal room. A conversation, then work.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you are looking for

    Trade, area, size, and whether you have already bought. We will be straight about what we know of and what we do not.

  2. 02

    Close with your own advisors

    Your counsel and due diligence run the purchase. We do not give legal or tax advice.

  3. 03

    Put Swyft HQ to work

    If the trade fits, a door-to-door team deploys after a permit check and housing setup. If office work is the bottleneck, Swyft International starts a placement.
