Quick answer

Swyft Business Broker is a business broker that adds value on both sides of a sale. Before you sell, Swyft HQ can put a trained door-to-door team on your service to add sold accounts, and Swyft International can place support or admin staff so the business leans on you less. After a purchase, the same services help the new owner grow. There are no listings here and no promised price or timeline. It starts with a call with Colin Speer.

For sellers

Raise the value first. Sell when you are ready.

Buyers pay for accounts, documented process, and a business that runs without the owner on every call. We work on those before the sale.

How it works for sellers
  1. 01

    Understand the business

    What you sell, where you sell it, how many accounts you carry, what you still do yourself each week, and how much more work your crews can take.

  2. 02

    Work the levers through Swyft HQ

    A door-to-door team sells your service on your paperwork, up to the capacity you set. Where it fits, Swyft International fills calling, support, bookkeeping, or admin seats.

  3. 03

    Run the sale conversation

    When you decide it is time, we talk through the sale. Your counsel and the usual due diligence are part of it.

For buyers

Bought the business. Now grow it.

A new owner has to learn the operation and grow it at the same time. Swyft HQ can carry the selling and some of the back-office work.

How it works for buyers
  1. 01

    Tell us what you are looking for

    The trade, the area, and the size of business you want. We will be straight with you about what we know of and what we do not.

  2. 02

    Close with your own advisors

    Counsel and due diligence stay with you and your advisors. We do not hand out legal or tax advice.

  3. 03

    Grow it after the purchase

    Swyft HQ can deploy a door-to-door team to add sold accounts and place offshore staff through Swyft International to take operational work off your plate.
