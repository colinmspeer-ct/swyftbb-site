For owners who want to sell

Make the business worth more before you sell it.

Buyers look at the accounts on the books, how the work gets sold, and how much of it depends on you. Those can change before a sale. Swyft HQ does that work.

Quick answer

We learn how your business runs, use Swyft HQ door-to-door teams and Swyft International placements to work on the parts a buyer looks at, and run the sale conversation when you say you are ready. We will not show you invented listings, and we will not promise a timeline or a price.

The sequence

Three steps, in order

The sale comes last. The value work comes first, and it is run by the same teams Swyft HQ deploys for its clients.

  1. 01

    Understand the business

    We start with a call. What you sell, the area you cover, how many accounts you carry, which ones renew, what your crews can handle, and which jobs still need you personally.

  2. 02

    Work the levers through Swyft HQ

    If your trade fits, Swyft HQ recruits, trains, houses, and manages a door-to-door team that sells your service on your paperwork. You keep the brand, the customer, and the install, service, and warranty work, and you set the capacity the team sells up to.

    If calls, support, bookkeeping, or admin keep you tied to the business, Swyft International can place offshore staff in those seats.

  3. 03

    Run the sale conversation when you are ready

    You decide when. A sale involves your own counsel and the usual due diligence. We do not give legal or tax advice.

How the door-to-door deal works

The same terms Swyft HQ uses with every client.

You keep the business

Reps sell on your paperwork under your brand. The customer is yours. Install, service, and warranty stay with your crews.

Revenue share per account

You pay a revenue share on each sold account. The rate is written in the agreement and is not published on this site or on Swyft HQ.

Deploy and reporting

Housing for the team and a check on the city's solicitation permit are part of deploy. You get a weekly report of doors, conversations, and sold accounts by rep.

Straight answers

What we will not do

No invented listings

There is no marketplace of businesses for sale on this site, and we will not show you sample listings or sample buyers.

No promised price or timeline

Results depend on your market, your crews, and the buyer. Anyone who quotes a number before seeing the business is guessing.

No borrowed proof

No client names, case studies, or result numbers we cannot back up. Ask on the call and we will tell you what we know.

Seller questions

Do I have to sell to use the Swyft HQ services?

No. Swyft HQ works with owners who have no plan to sell. The broker side only matters when you decide you want to.

Will you list my business somewhere?

Not without talking to you first. Nothing about your business goes public from a contact form or a first call.

What does it cost?

The door-to-door rate is a revenue share per sold account, written in the agreement. Swyft International charges a success fee on placements, published on its page. Broker terms are discussed directly on a call.

Do I need a lawyer?

A sale of a business normally involves counsel and due diligence on both sides. We are not your lawyer and do not give legal advice.
