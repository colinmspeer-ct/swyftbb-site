About

A broker backed by a team that sells.

Swyft Business Broker is the business-broker offer of Swyft (Swyft Solutions LLC). Colin Speer leads the company.

Quick answer

Swyft Business Broker is run by Swyft, the same company behind Swyft HQ. The value work before a sale and the growth work after a purchase are done by Swyft HQ's door-to-door teams and, where it fits, Swyft International placements. Contact is by email or phone.

Who does the work

One company, two offers

Swyft HQ recruits, trains, houses, and manages door-to-door reps who sell a client's service on the client's paperwork. The client keeps the brand, the customer, and the install, service, and warranty work. It runs in exteriors and roofing, window cleaning, pest control, lawn care, fiber internet and wireless, interior home improvement, home security, water treatment, and HVAC.

Swyft International places offshore staff in seats that do not need a truck: appointment setting, customer support, bookkeeping, and admin.

Swyft Business Broker puts those services to work around a sale: raising value before an owner sells, and growing the business after a buyer buys.

What we will not put on this site

If we cannot back it up, it does not go up.

No borrowed client names

No logos, testimonials, or case studies that are not ours to show.

No result numbers

No deal counts, revenue multiples, or growth figures. Ask on a call and you will get a straight answer.

No rate card for the door-to-door deal

No fake listings

There is no public marketplace of businesses for sale here.

No license claims

We do not claim a broker license on this site. A sale involves your own counsel and the usual due diligence.
